The Hidden Risk

Why Permission Creep Matters

When you first install an app, you review its permissions. But over dozens of routine background updates, apps quietly request broader access to your device. Most users never notice when an photo editing app suddenly gains background microphone access or contact list reading permissions. HawkEye exposes this invisible privilege escalation.
